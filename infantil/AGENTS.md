# Infantil DOX

## Purpose

- Governs the Congressinho Espírita page (`/infantil`), the children's programming of the 43º Congresso Espírita de Goiás (2027).

## Ownership

- Owned files: `infantil/index.html`, `infantil/styles.css`.
- Shared assets: `imagens/logomarca-congressinho-espirita.jpg`, `imagens/logomarca-congressinho-espirita.webp`, `imagens/simbolo-acessibilidade-badge.png`.

## Local Contracts

- Maintains the typography and architectural hierarchy of the root website (Cormorant Garamond and Manrope).
- Embraces enhanced color freedom and playful child-friendly styling suited for families and children.
- Clearly communicates dates, schedules, and locations for parents:
  - 6 a 8/02/2027, sábado a segunda-feira.
  - 06/02/2027, sábado, 18h–21h: sala de apoio para crianças a partir de 6 anos de idade, no Centro de Convenções (vagas limitadas).
  - 07 e 08/02/2027, domingo e segunda-feira: Atividades no Lar Francisca de Lima.
  - Segunda-feira, 08/02/2027: Apresentação Musical no Palco do Centro de Convenções (o traslado é responsabilidade dos pais).
  - Detailed schedules announced opportunely.
- Incorporates the 4 brand symbols: A Semente, O Caminho, O Coração, A Árvore.
- Show `imagens/logomarca-congressinho-espirita` complete, without crop, frame, overlay, or caption.
- Features the Universal Symbol of Accessibility aligned with the FEB inclusive perspective. Use `imagens/simbolo-universal-acessibilidade.png` for that mark. Do not substitute the wheelchair emoji.
- Direct registration links to `https://evento.digital/feego/43cego`.

## Work Guidance

- Keep copy warm, welcoming, and reassuring for parents and guardians.
- Ensure all interactive elements remain accessible, responsive, and conformant with `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Verification

- Preview locally with `npm run dev` or `node dev-server.mjs` at `http://localhost:3000/infantil/`.
- Validate responsive layouts on desktop and mobile viewports.
